ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

About FON 4 TECH

FON 4 TECH

Empowering people with practical digital knowledge, modern technology skills and innovative approaches relevant to today's digital economy.

FON 4 TECH is a digital skills development initiative designed to expose and equip people in Ebonyi State with practical knowledge, modern technology skills and innovative approaches relevant to today's digital economy.

Hosted under the leadership of the Ebonyi State Government and implemented through the Ebonyi State Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency (EBSMEDA), the programme brings together people with a shared interest in technology, creativity, innovation and entrepreneurship.

FON 4 TECH represents a commitment to creating opportunities for people to learn, experiment, create and develop the skills required to participate meaningfully in an increasingly digital world.

02
WHY FON 4 TECH

Our Purpose

Technology is changing the way the world learns, works, communicates and conducts business.

FON 4 TECH was created to give people the opportunity to understand these changes and develop practical skills that can be applied beyond the classroom.

Through an intensive technology-focused training programme, participants are introduced to contemporary digital tools, concepts and workflows across four major areas of technology.

The programme encourages participants not only to learn, but also to practise, create, collaborate and solve problems using the knowledge they acquire.

WHAT WE TEACH

Our Four Areas of Training

FON 4 TECH brings together four important areas of modern digital technology.

01
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI Automation

Artificial intelligence is transforming industries and changing how people work.

Participants are introduced to AI-powered tools and automation concepts that can improve productivity, streamline repetitive tasks and support the development of modern digital solutions.

The training provides an opportunity for participants to understand how artificial intelligence can be applied to practical problems and everyday workflows.

02
DIGITAL DEVELOPMENT

Web Development

The web is one of the most important platforms for communication, business and digital services.

The Web Development track introduces participants to the fundamentals of creating websites and web-based applications.

Participants gain practical exposure to technologies and tools used to build functional, responsive and user-focused digital experiences, while developing an understanding of how ideas can be transformed into working websites.

03
CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY

Graphic Design

Visual communication plays an important role in today's digital world.

The Graphic Design track develops participants' creative and technical abilities in creating visual content for businesses, brands, digital platforms and other forms of communication.

Participants are encouraged to combine creativity, design principles and technology to communicate ideas effectively through visual media.

04
EMERGING TECHNOLOGY

Web3

Web3 represents an emerging area of technology built around new approaches to digital ownership, decentralised systems and online interaction.

Participants are introduced to fundamental Web3 concepts and the technologies shaping this evolving digital ecosystem.

The training provides an opportunity to explore emerging ideas and understand how the next generation of internet technologies may create new possibilities for individuals, businesses and communities.

THE FON 4 TECH EXPERIENCE

A Week of Learning, Creativity and Innovation

FON 4 TECH is structured as an intensive technology training experience designed to deliver meaningful exposure to digital skills within a focused period.

Throughout the programme, participants engage with technology through practical learning, demonstrations, exercises, collaboration and project-based activities.

The experience goes beyond simply listening to lectures. Participants are encouraged to take what they learn and turn it into practical work.

From writing and structuring HTML and CSS to working with WordPress, exploring artificial intelligence, understanding Web3 concepts and creating visual designs, participants are exposed to different approaches to building and working with technology.

This hands-on approach helps transform technology from something participants simply use into something they can understand, create with and apply.

HOW WE LEARN

Our Approach

At the heart of FON 4 TECH is practical learning.

Participants are encouraged to move beyond theory and engage directly with the tools and technologies they are learning.

The programme combines instruction with practical activities, allowing participants to experiment with new ideas, develop projects, collaborate with others and apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios.

This approach is intended to build not only technical knowledge, but also creativity, problem-solving, confidence and digital awareness.
